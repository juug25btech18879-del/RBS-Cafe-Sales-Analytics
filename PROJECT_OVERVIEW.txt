PROJECT TITLE: RBS Cafe – Retail Sales Management & Analytics Dashboard

OBJECTIVE:
To develop a desktop-based retail sales application for a small cafe that records orders,
maintains menu items, stores sales in CSV files, and presents useful sales analytics.

MODULES:
1. Dashboard
2. New Billing
3. Sales Records
4. Analytics
5. Menu Management
6. Reports

TECHNOLOGIES:
Python, Tkinter, Pandas, NumPy, CSV, Matplotlib

DATA STORAGE:
Local CSV files are created automatically in the data folder on first execution.
